[ INSTALLATION GUIDE ]

Saddle up.

Eight steps from key to ride. Pick your menu, follow the trail, the wraith answers when called.

SECTION 01Pick your menu

Choose your menu.

Wraith currently rides exclusively on Scooby Menu. Other base menus are on the trail — we'll list them here as compatibility lands.

SECTION 02The trail

Eight steps.

01

Buy your license key.

Pick a tier on the buy page — Elite or Platinum. Pay once. Your license key is delivered instantly to the email you used at checkout.

NOTE
Check your spam folder if the key doesn't arrive within 60 seconds.
Go to store →

02

Register or sign in.

If you already have a Scooby account, just sign in with it — no need to make a second one. New rider? Register a fresh account in under a minute.

NOTE
Your Wraith license is bound to this account. Keep your credentials safe.

Redeem the key.

Open your dashboard and paste the license key you received. Wraith will be tied to your account — no further activation needed.

04

Load Scooby in-game.

Launch RedM, join your server, and inject Scooby Menu with your executor. Wait for the Scooby UI to appear.

NOTE
Your executor must already have Scooby auto-loading. If not, see Scooby's own setup docs first.

05

Open Lua tab & Library subtab.

Inside the Scooby UI, navigate to the Lua tab, then click the Library subtab. This is where your installed scripts and extensions appear.

NOTE
The Library subtab is inside the Lua tab — look for it in the secondary row once Lua is open.

06

Go to Premium Menus.

Inside the Lua tab, find the Premium Menus subtab. Wraith and other licensed extensions live here.

NOTE
If "Premium Menus" doesn't appear, your Scooby account isn't linked yet — go back to step 03 and confirm your license is redeemed.

07

Find Wraith. Click View.

Locate Wraith in the Premium Menus list and hit the View button next to it. This brings up the load panel.

TIER
Whether you bought Elite or Platinum, the entry shows up the same — your license tier is checked at load time.

08

Press Load Script.

Hit Load Script. The wraith arrives. Your menu unlocks and you're free to ride. Welcome to the frontier.

FIRST RUN
Initial load takes ~2 seconds while the model library indexes. After that it's instant.
